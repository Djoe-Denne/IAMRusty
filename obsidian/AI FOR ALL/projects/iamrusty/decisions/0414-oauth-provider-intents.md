# ADR 0414 — OAuth provider intents

[Read the proposed ADR](https://github.com/Djoe-Denne/IAMRusty/blob/master/docs/adr/0414-oauth-provider-intents.md)
and its [observable contract](https://github.com/Djoe-Denne/IAMRusty/blob/master/docs/contracts/oauth-provider-intents.md).
The proposal separates provider Login, Signup, and explicit Link while preserving
OAuthConnect and browser binding. Publication does not ratify it or authorize
implementation.
